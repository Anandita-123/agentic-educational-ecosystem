# Chunk 2: Application & Registration Process

### Step 1: Form Availability

Application forms will be available through the college login for all enrolled UG and PG regular and backlog students.

### Step 2: Student Submission

After colleges release forms online, students can submit applications through their login credentials.

If there are internet/connectivity issues, college administration may assist students in submitting applications upon request.

### Step 3: Examination Fee Payment

After form submission, students must pay examination fees to their respective colleges.

### Step 4: Separate Forms

Regular and backlog examination forms must be filled out separately.

### Step 5: University Fee Submission

Colleges will submit examination fees to the University in a consolidated manner.

### Step 6: Signed Forms

Collection and maintenance of signed forms will be handled by colleges. These may need to be submitted to the University later if requested.

### Step 7: Admit Card

After successful fee submission, admit cards will be generated online through the student login portal.

# Chunk 3: Examination Information

### Practical / Sessional Examinations

* Conducted at respective colleges.
* Colleges will publish schedules separately.
* Marks submission facilities will be available only for students whose admit cards have been generated.

### Theory Examinations

* Conducted at respective colleges.
* Duration: **3 Hours**
* Full Marks: **70**
* Sessions:

  * Morning: **10:00 AM – 1:00 PM**
  * Afternoon: **2:00 PM – 5:00 PM**

### Answer Scripts

All theory examinations will use specially designed answer booklets containing **24 pages**.

Students are advised to prepare accordingly.
